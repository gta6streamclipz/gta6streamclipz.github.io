A personal tool for posting approved GTA gameplay highlight clips.

About

gta6streamclipz is a private tool used only by its operator, Alex. It is not a public product, has no sign-up, and is not used by anyone else. Alex reviews and approves each clip himself, then the tool posts it to his own accounts using official platform APIs.

How it works

  • Collect short gameplay highlight clips
  • Review and approve those clips before anything is posted
  • Publish approved clips to the gta6streamclipz accounts on TikTok, YouTube, and Instagram
