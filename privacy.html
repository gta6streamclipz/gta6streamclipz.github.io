Privacy Policy

gta6streamclipz
Effective date: November 13, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the gta6streamclipz application (the "App") handles information. The App is a personal tool used only by its operator, Alex, to upload short gameplay highlight clips that he edits and approves himself to his own social accounts:

  • TikTok: @gta6streamclipz
  • YouTube: @GTA6StreamClipz
  • Instagram: gta6.streamclipz

Contact: gta6streamclipz.yt@gmail.com

YouTube API Services

The App uses YouTube API Services to upload videos to the operator's own YouTube channel, @GTA6StreamClipz. By using the App, you are agreeing to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service.

Information Google collects when the App uses YouTube API Services is explained in the Google Privacy Policy.

The App accesses only the YouTube data needed to upload clips to that channel: upload capability, and the channel ID and channel name. That information is stored only to operate the tool. The App does not access other YouTube channels or other users' YouTube data.

Access to the YouTube account can be revoked at any time on Google's security settings page (Third-party access / permissions). After access is revoked or a deletion request is made, the App's YouTube tokens are deleted, and any stored YouTube API data is deleted within 30 days. To request deletion, email gta6streamclipz.yt@gmail.com.

1. Who this policy covers

The App is not used by the public. It does not collect information from other people, and it does not have end users besides Alex.

2. Information the App stores

The App stores OAuth access and refresh tokens for Alex's own TikTok, YouTube, and Instagram accounts so it can post approved clips on his behalf. Those tokens are stored only to operate the tool. The App does not collect, store, or process personal data from anyone else.

The App may also keep records needed to run the tool, such as clip files Alex has already reviewed and post status for his own accounts. Clip files are stored in the operator's private cloud storage (Vercel Blob).

3. How that information is used

Tokens and local records are used only to operate the App for Alex: authenticating to official platform APIs and publishing clips he has already approved to his own accounts.

4. Sharing and selling

The App does not sell, rent, or share personal data with third parties. Tokens are sent only to the official APIs of the platforms they belong to (for example, YouTube, TikTok, or Instagram) in order to authenticate and publish content.

5. Access, revocation, and deletion

Alex can revoke the App's access at any time in the settings for each connected platform:

  • YouTube / Google: Google security settings (Third-party access / permissions)
  • TikTok: Settings > Security and permissions, or the equivalent Connected apps / Authorized apps section
  • Instagram / Meta: Meta Accounts Center, or Instagram Settings > Accounts Center > Password and security > Apps and websites (or the equivalent connected apps section)

Revoking access stops the App from using those tokens. Tokens and stored API data can also be deleted by requesting deletion at gta6streamclipz.yt@gmail.com, or by deleting them from the systems that run the App. After revocation or a deletion request, tokens are deleted and any stored API data is deleted within 30 days.

6. Data retention

Tokens are kept only while the App needs them to post to Alex's accounts. They are deleted if access is revoked, if a deletion request is made, or if they are removed from the systems that run the App. Any stored API data is deleted within 30 days of revocation or a deletion request.

Clip files are stored in the operator's private cloud storage (Vercel Blob) and are deleted 7 days after a clip is posted or rejected.

7. Changes

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. The effective date at the top of this page will be updated when that happens.

8. Contact

Questions about this Privacy Policy can be sent to gta6streamclipz.yt@gmail.com.
