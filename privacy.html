Privacy Policy

gta6streamclipz
Effective date: November 12, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the gta6streamclipz application (the "App") handles information. The App is a personal tool used only by its operator, Alex, to upload short gameplay highlight clips that he edits and approves himself to his own social accounts:

  • TikTok: @gta6streamclipz
  • YouTube: @gta6streamclipz
  • Instagram: gta6.streamclipz

Contact: gta6streamclipz.yt@gmail.com

1. Who this policy covers

The App is not used by the public. It does not collect information from other people, and it does not have end users besides Alex.

2. Information the App stores

The App stores OAuth access and refresh tokens for Alex's own TikTok, YouTube, and Instagram accounts so it can post approved clips on his behalf. Those tokens are stored locally on the machine that runs the App. The App does not collect, store, or process personal data from anyone else.

The App may also keep local records needed to run the tool, such as clip files Alex has already reviewed and post status for his own accounts.

3. How that information is used

Tokens and local records are used only to operate the App for Alex: authenticating to official platform APIs and publishing clips he has already approved to his own accounts.

4. Sharing and selling

The App does not sell, rent, or share personal data with third parties. Tokens are sent only to the official APIs of the platforms they belong to (for example, TikTok) in order to authenticate and publish content.

5. Access, revocation, and deletion

Alex can revoke the App's access at any time in his TikTok account settings (Settings > Security and permissions, or the equivalent Connected apps / Authorized apps section). Revoking access stops the App from using those tokens. Tokens and local records can also be deleted from the machine that runs the App.

6. Data retention

Tokens are kept only while the App needs them to post to Alex's accounts. They are removed if access is revoked or if they are deleted locally.

7. Changes

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. The effective date at the top of this page will be updated when that happens.

8. Contact

Questions about this Privacy Policy can be sent to gta6streamclipz.yt@gmail.com.
